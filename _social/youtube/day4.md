**AFRICAST 2026 – Day 4: Exponential smoothing and forecast accuracy**

This is the Day 4 recording of AFRICAST 2026, a free online course in tidy time series analysis and forecasting with R. It was run by Forecasting for Social Good (F4SG) from 19 to 23 October 2026.

📈 **Session 1: ETS (exponential smoothing)** (Bahman Rostami-Tabar, Cardiff University)
Exponential smoothing models for level, trend and seasonality:
• From simple methods to exponential smoothing, and Pegels' classification
• Simple exponential smoothing with additive and multiplicative errors
• Holt's linear trend and damped trend methods
• Holt–Winters additive and multiplicative seasonal methods
• The ETS taxonomy, estimation and model selection with AIC, AICc and BIC
• Non-Gaussian forecast distributions, transformations and bootstrapping

🎯 **Session 2: Basic training and test accuracy** (Bahman Rostami-Tabar, Cardiff University)
How to tell whether your forecasts are any good:
• In-sample (training) vs out-of-sample (test) evaluation
• Forecast errors and point accuracy measures, including MASE and RMSSE
• Evaluating prediction intervals with the Winkler score
• Evaluating forecast distributions with quantile scores and CRPS

🌍 **About AFRICAST**
AFRICAST is part of the Forecasting for Social Good (F4SG) initiative. It builds forecasting skills among researchers in Africa so that forecasts can inform planning and resource allocation. The project is led by Bahman Rostami-Tabar (Cardiff University). The instructors are Mitchell O'Hara-Wild, Abiodun Egwuenu, Harsha Halgamuwe Hewage and Mustafa Aslan, and Henry Kissinger Ochieng leads coordination. Learn more at https://www.f4sg.org/
