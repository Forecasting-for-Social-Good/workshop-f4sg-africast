**AFRICAST 2026 – Day 3: Time series regression and ARIMA**

This is the Day 3 recording of AFRICAST 2026, a free online course in tidy time series analysis and forecasting with R. It was run by Forecasting for Social Good (F4SG) from 19 to 23 October 2026.

📉 **Session 1: Time series regression** (Mitchell O'Hara-Wild, Monash University)
How to forecast using extra information with linear regression models:
• Simple and multiple linear regression for time series
• Residual diagnostics for regression models
• Selecting predictors with cross-validation, AIC and AICc
• Ex-ante vs ex-post forecasts and scenario-based forecasting
• Correlation, causation, multicollinearity and outliers
• Useful predictors: dummy and intervention variables, interactions, and lagged and lead predictors

📊 **Session 2: ARIMA** (Mustafa Aslan, Cardiff University)
An introduction to ARIMA models and how `fable` selects them automatically:
• Stationarity and differencing
• Autoregressive (AR), moving average (MA) and ARIMA models
• How `ARIMA()` works: the Hyndman–Khandakar algorithm
• Seasonal ARIMA, with corticosteroid drug sales as a worked example
• Combining models with forecast ensembles

🌍 **About AFRICAST**
AFRICAST is part of the Forecasting for Social Good (F4SG) initiative. It builds forecasting skills among researchers in Africa so that forecasts can inform planning and resource allocation. The project is led by Bahman Rostami-Tabar (Cardiff University). The instructors are Mitchell O'Hara-Wild, Abiodun Egwuenu, Harsha Halgamuwe Hewage and Mustafa Aslan, and Henry Kissinger Ochieng leads coordination. Learn more at https://www.f4sg.org/
